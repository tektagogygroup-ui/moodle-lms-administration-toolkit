**Document type:** Digital Learning Quality Assurance  
**Area:** Moodle Learning Management System  
**Prepared by:** TEKTAGOGY Group  
**Purpose:** Professional portfolio demonstration

## 1. Purpose

This checklist provides a structured approach to reviewing the quality of Moodle administration, digital learning delivery and LMS support processes.

It is informed by the quality management principles and process approach of ISO 9001:2015. It is a sample portfolio resource, not an official ISO audit checklist or a certification assessment instrument.

## 3. Audit Checklist

### A. LMS Governance and Responsibilities

- [ ] LMS administration responsibilities are clearly assigned.
- [ ] Roles and permissions are appropriately defined.
- [ ] Procedures for managing courses and users are documented.
- [ ] Relevant staff understand their LMS responsibilities.
- [ ] Escalation procedures for technical issues are established.

### B. Course Management and Configuration

- [ ] Course creation follows an established procedure.
- [ ] Course naming and categorisation are consistent.
- [ ] Course access and visibility settings are reviewed.
- [ ] Lecturer and student enrolments are appropriately managed.
- [ ] Course closure and archiving procedures are defined.

### C. Learning Content and Accessibility

- [ ] Learning materials are organised consistently.
- [ ] Resources are accessible to intended learners.
- [ ] Links and uploaded files are checked.
- [ ] Learning content is reviewed periodically.
- [ ] Procedures exist for correcting outdated or inaccurate content.

### D. Assessment and Online Examination Management

- [ ] Quiz settings are reviewed before assessment opens.
- [ ] Assessment availability and timing are appropriate.
- [ ] Question banks are managed appropriately.
- [ ] Assignment submission and grading procedures are documented.
- [ ] Student assessment issues are recorded and investigated.
- [ ] Appropriate controls protect assessment integrity.

### E. User Support and Competence

- [ ] Lecturers receive appropriate Moodle training.
- [ ] Students receive guidance on using the LMS.
- [ ] Support requests are recorded.
- [ ] Support issues are tracked to resolution.
- [ ] Recurring problems inform future training and improvements.

### F. Monitoring and Performance Evaluation

- [ ] LMS usage and activity reports are reviewed.
- [ ] Course readiness checks are conducted.
- [ ] User feedback is collected where appropriate.
- [ ] Common technical issues are analysed.
- [ ] Quality indicators are reviewed periodically.

### G. Corrective Action and Continual Improvement

- [ ] Identified issues are documented.
- [ ] Root causes are investigated where appropriate.
- [ ] Corrective actions are assigned to responsible persons.
- [ ] Implementation of corrective actions is monitored.
- [ ] Effectiveness of completed actions is reviewed.
- [ ] Lessons learned inform improvements to LMS processes.
