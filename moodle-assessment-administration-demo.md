# Moodle Assessment Administration: Practical Demonstration

## 1. Overview

This document demonstrates practical knowledge of Moodle assessment administration through guided exploration of the official Mount Orange Moodle demonstration site.

The demonstration focused on assignment and quiz administration within the **Celebrating Cultures** course, using the Lecturer role.

The activities explored key Moodle features used to support lecturers in managing online assessments, monitoring submissions, grading learners, and configuring quizzes.

## 2. Assignment Administration

The assignment activity explored was **Languages of Love**.

The following functions were reviewed:

- Assignment settings and deadline configuration.
- Submission management and monitoring.
- Filtering submissions by status.
- Reviewing learner submission information.
- Quick grading and grade entry.
- Providing feedback through the online text editor.
- Managing assessment submissions through the lecturer interface.

These functions support effective administration and monitoring of online coursework.

## 3. Quiz Administration

The quiz activity explored was **Know your Geography!**

The following configuration areas were reviewed.

### 3.1 Timing settings

- Quiz opening date.
- Quiz due date.
- Quiz closing date.
- Time limit.
- Behaviour when the time limit expires.

### 3.2 Grade settings

- Grade category.
- Grade required to pass.
- Number of attempts allowed.
- Grading method, including the highest-grade option.

### 3.3 Question behaviour

- Shuffling questions within the quiz.
- Interactive behaviour with multiple tries.
- Allowing learners to redo questions within an attempt.
- Configuring whether each attempt builds on the previous attempt.

### 3.4 Review options

The review settings were explored to understand how lecturers control the information learners can see:

- Whether their responses were correct.
- Marks awarded.
- Specific and general feedback.
- Correct answers.
- Overall feedback.

These options help lecturers align quiz feedback with assessment objectives and learning requirements.

### 3.5 Question management

The Questions interface was explored, including:

- Viewing individual quiz questions.
- Reviewing marks assigned to questions.
- Accessing the option to add questions.

## 4. Professional Competencies Demonstrated

The practical exploration covered the following Moodle administration competencies:

- Online assignment administration.
- Assessment submission monitoring.
- Grading and feedback management.
- Quiz configuration and administration.
- Question management.
- Assessment timing and grading configuration.
- Review and feedback settings.
- Understanding the learner assessment experience.
- Supporting lecturers in the use of Moodle assessment tools.

## 5. Evidence and Scope

This demonstration was conducted using the publicly accessible Mount Orange Moodle demonstration environment.

It is a practical portfolio demonstration of Moodle functionality. It should not be interpreted as evidence of changes made to a live institutional Moodle installation.

No institutional assessment records or identifiable learner information are included in this document.

## 6. Professional Relevance

The demonstration complements practical experience in Moodle LMS administration, lecturer support, online assessment support, and learning management system quality assurance.

It illustrates familiarity with Moodle's assessment tools and the configuration options available to lecturers and LMS administrators.
